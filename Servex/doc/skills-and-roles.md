## Where each count comes from

- **Skill calls:** `.claude/hooks/ledger.mjs` writes `{"log":{"at","msg":"skill: <name>"}}` into the active task's `task.jsonl` on every Skill call. The script reads every `task.jsonl` and `session.jsonl` under `public/`. It cannot see a tab or session with no open task (it logs nothing), a plugin skill that is not in the log under its plain name, or any use before the task existed.
- **Role spawns and inlined skills:** Servex appends one `skills` line per spawn to `%LOCALAPPDATA%/lew42/servex/logs/skills.jsonl` (`Servex/agents/Agents.js`): `role`, and `injected`, the names of the skill and role texts put in the first message. An inlined skill was loaded into the prompt, not necessarily used by the agent. This log is on this PC only and starts at 2026-10-12T03:14:58-05:00 (2001 lines).
- The 30-day window is therefore really the days since that date for role spawns (about 24 days), and the ledger logs only since each task.jsonl exists.
- The catalogue is the folders in `.claude/skills/` that hold a `SKILL.md` (35) and the rows of the table in `Servex/agents/roles.js` plus the files in `Servex/agents/roles/` (20 names).
