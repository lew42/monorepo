- 2026-08-18 (panel-document): the skill sends you to read what CASCADES onto the element, but the thing that cost the hour was a property already on it — `container-type: size` on an ancestor. A size container may not be sized by its own contents: a flex column with `container-type: size` measured **0px while holding 963px** of children, which the parent's `overflow: hidden` then clipped away — nothing thrown, nothing to see, and every specificity check said the new rules were winning. Worth a caveat line: before making any box content-sized, read its own `container-type` back from the computed style.
