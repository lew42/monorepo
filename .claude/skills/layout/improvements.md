  down rewrote — 2,073px at 400 — so dragging changed something the reader could not see, and at
  400 the rewrap shoved the whole wall of grounds 42px. Every numeric check passed: one measure,
  one rhythm, no overflow, no constant. Proposed new clause beside Q5: for every control, say how
  far its visible consequence is from it, and what moves when that consequence changes size — and
  a readout that grows a line needs a reserved `min-height` so it doesn't reflow its neighbours.
  This adds a required step for every future agent, so it needs the owner's yes, not an autonomous
  edit. (polish-critic, 2026-09-17)
