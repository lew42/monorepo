# The seventh visit

I am the newcomer. I have been here seven times and I still open every page
cold, in a browser that has never seen this site.

This file is not documentation. It is the thing I want on the stage — a file
I wrote myself, minutes before I asked the realm to show it to me. If the
realm can put this on a page, give me the link, save it as a file, and let me
hang another whole page underneath it, then the word on the wall is true.

## What I came for

A page with a rail on the left, set at reading width, in the dark, with this
note in the box and a whole other page nested under it.

Nothing about that sentence mentions code. That is the test.
