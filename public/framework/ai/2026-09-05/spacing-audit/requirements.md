2. **You collect** the four json files into one ranked list: the discrepancies (a ratio with no reason; a control that is a strip; a page that does not grow), and the uncertainties (where two auditors disagree, or where the rule is missing).
3. **One Fable judge, foreground**, with your ranked list and the pictures (paths). The judge WRITES the decision (`/imagine/design/spacing/decision.md`, §33 shape, one screen) and APPLIES it: the tokens in `framework.css` `:root`, the column pads in `core/Page/Page.css`, the toolbars page's strip (`public/imagine/paging/toolbars/`), and any realm-level constants the auditors found (each with the numbers in a comment). The judge is the owner's stand-in today: decide, document, keep the alternatives on the ceilings page.
