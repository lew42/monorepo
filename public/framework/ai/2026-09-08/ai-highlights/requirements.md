## The design

1. **A highlight is one line a task carries** — appended to its own `task.jsonl` as `{"assign": {"highlight": {"icon": "<Material Icons name>", "title": "<five words or fewer>", "line": "<one plain sentence a newcomer follows>", "url": "<the thing itself, e.g. /layouts/>"}}}`. The task record stays the one source of truth (nothing crawls; no separate curated file). `stats.js` / the manifest reader surfaces it as `m.highlight`. Document the field in `doc/manifest.md`, and add ONE optional sentence to `.claude/skills/finish-task/SKILL.md` §3: a landing that produced something the owner will look for later adds `highlight` (icon · title · line · url) — optional, not a new required step.
2. **The front, `/framework/ai/`, by default:** the active strip first (what is running right now — "especially current/in-flight"), then the usage rail as today, then **Highlights**: a wall of cards, newest first, each an icon, the title, the sentence, the date, the card linking to `url` (the thing) with a small link to the task page; one screen at 1920 — if more than ~24, a "Show more" (no persistence). Then one line: "Everything, newest first → `/framework/ai/log/`". The day rail and the effort pills move to `/log/` (or stay as one compact row — your call, say why in the log); the date pages `/framework/ai/<date>/` and `/effort/<slug>/` are unchanged.
3. **`/framework/ai/log/`:** every task of every day down one spine, in-flight first, then newest first — the spine `rail()` draws today — rendered **40 at a time** with a "Show more" button (state in memory only; a refresh starts over). `ai/page.js` gains the `log` route the same way `effort` has one, or a declared child with its own `page.js` — read `new-page` and pick the one that keeps the day route intact.
4. **The backfill — the part that makes it useful on the first open.** Read every `landed` line in `public/framework/ai/*/day.jsonl` (one line per task, ~500) and every landed task's `outcome` headline (the bold first line — `card.js` already extracts it), and mark the major accomplishments: something the owner would go looking for — a tier, a realm, a system, a class, a standard, a post, a tool, a study with a page. Not fixes, audits, scouts, critics, slices of a larger thing (mark the thing once, on the task that landed it), or runs (a mastermind run is a container; mark its products). Aim for 30–50 across the 24 days; when in doubt, leave it out — the log has everything. Write each `highlight` line through a script (`ai-highlights-mark.mjs` in the scratchpad: JSON via `JSON.stringify`, pure ASCII, appended with `fs.appendFileSync`, and re-parse every line of each file after), never by hand. The seven the owner is looking for right now must be among them, with these urls: `2026-09-01/themes-browser` → `/imagine/design/themes/`; `2026-09-01/controls-study` → `/imagine/design/controls/`; `2026-09-01/color-study` → `/imagine/design/color/`; `2026-09-06/ui-theme` → `/framework/ui/controls/`; `2026-08-30/color-stacks` → `/framework/styles/stacks/`; `2026-08-21/ux-system-plan` → `/framework/ux/`; `2026-09-08/layouts-standard` → `/layouts/`, `websites-db` → `/websites/`, `blog-playwright` → `/blog/ai/playwright/`. Log the full list you marked as `log` lines (date · slug · title) so the owner can flip any.
5. **Icons**: Material Icons ligature names already used across the site (`rg 'icon: "' public/ --glob '*.js' -o | sort | uniq -c | sort -rn | head -40` shows the vocabulary); one icon per kind of thing — a tier, a study, a post, a tool — consistently, so the wall reads at a glance.
