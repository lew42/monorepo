# imagine-research — review one realm

One of eighteen reviewers in the [imagine-review](../imagine-review/) wave, 2026-09-04. This one
took **/imagine/research/**.

The ask was the same for all eighteen and is written once, in full, at
[`../imagine-review/reviewer-brief.md`](../imagine-review/reviewer-brief.md): open the realm cold
on a private server at 1280 and 3440, write down what a stranger would say the page is for and
what the page's own code says it is for, prove the first thing a reader would click with a
`ui-test`, check the three layout invariants at 3440 — and then **fix what you find** rather
than filing it, keeping each fix easy to change and writing its caveat beside it.

The findings, the fixes and the caveats are in `task.jsonl`, on this page. All eighteen are read
together at [/imagine/review/](/imagine/review/).
