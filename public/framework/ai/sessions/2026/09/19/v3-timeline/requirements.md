> New idea for the log: a graphical timeline. Events are placed spatially so distance shows time apart, on an adaptive scale: an hour mark always (4:00, 5:00), minute marks only above and below where events land, seconds placing an event within its minute. It auto-scrolls as time passes. Long empty gaps collapse to a "4:00 ... 6:00" break and the timeline restarts, so you sense days and hours without huge blank stretches.

Exact times are not crucial; the goal is natural temporal awareness: flow and what is current.

## Build

1. **Full bleed, two even columns.** The V3 page fills everything right of the site sidebar: zero page padding, no empty top band (find what holds the ~80 px above the head today and the side gutters — the catalog parent's `--gutter-x`/`--pad-y`, `.bleed`; the V2 task solved the same thing with a `:has()` rule on the outer page — read `ai/v/2/v2.css`). Two columns share the width evenly, the existing drag handle between them, each column its own full-height scroller (the scrollbar runs the column's whole height), each with the DEFAULT section pad (`var(--pad)` — try it first; judge by the shot at 1920 and 3440; say what you kept).
2. **The left column is a TIMELINE, newest at the top.** Same items as the inbox (one per card id, owner cards included), but placed on a time axis:
   - an **hour line** for every hour that has events (and the current hour), labelled (`4 PM`), running the column's full width BEHIND the cards;
   - **minute lines** only near events — a faint line at each minute that holds an event, labelled (`4:31`) in a left margin/gutter; **each card's time lives there, OUTSIDE the card**, on its own line — the card face loses its time;
   - **distance shows time, adaptively**: within a busy stretch the vertical gap between two items grows with the minutes between them, clamped (say 0.5em per minute, max ~6em) so a quiet ten minutes is visibly longer than a busy one without wasting a screen; a gap over ~45 minutes **collapses to a break** ("4:00 … 6:00", a torn/dashed line) — so an overnight gap is one row;
   - a **now line** at the top that moves as time passes (update once a minute; no animation loop), so "what is current" is felt;
   - the grid lines may be drawn with the landed background layer (`public/framework/ui/background/` — read its readme; a `background(() => …)` of absolutely placed lines behind a `.background-content` wrapper) or plain positioned elements — choose the smaller; say which.
   - Selection, routing (`/v/3/<id>/`), "never rebuild the list", "a new item never moves the owner's scroll or selection" all stay exactly as they are. This REPLACES the plain inbox as the `timeline` view; the `now` and `grid` views stay.
3. **New item, and the jump key.** A new item lands at the top without moving anything; a small **"New item" button** appears; **Spacebar** (when no input has focus) jumps to the newest item and selects it — StarCraft's "jump to last alert"; press again to go back to where you were.
4. **Prompt cards.** A card line may carry `ask: ["yes", "no"]` (any short labels): the card renders those buttons; pressing one appends `{"card": {"id": <same id>, "answer": "<label>", "author": "owner", "status": "done"}}` to the board through the dev socket's append RPC (find how the dev bar composer appends), and the card shows the answer. Add `--ask "yes,no"` to `.claude/skills/every-prompt/say.mjs` (a flag beside `--focus`) so the mastermind and the assistant can prompt the owner from the dashboard.
5. **The usage footer.** One slim footer across both columns with the usage bars the old AI dashboard shows (session and week: used against elapsed — find the component in `ext/AITask/dashboard.js` / `stats.js`; reuse it, do not redraw it; it reads `/framework/ai/usage.json`).
6. **Resurfacing** — a card updated or referenced again (a newer owner card mentions its id or title) moves to its new time on the axis (it already re-sorts by last update — make the move visible with a brief highlight), and a card may embed a one-line preview of another card (`see: "<card-id>"` → icon + title + status, a link to its route).

7. **Important things must not sink** (the owner, 17:10: "important items get pushed down by streams, so it needs prioritization, a Live toggle button near the top, and a toolbar (asked earlier, never appeared)"; "a newly arriving card should select itself unless I have selected something"). In the timeline view: a small PINNED strip above the axis holding every `needs-you` card and the newest `focus` card, whatever their time — they also stay at their place on the axis; a **Live** toggle in the head row (on: the view follows and auto-selects each newly arriving card UNLESS the owner has selected something themselves this visit or is scrolled away — then the "New item" button and Spacebar do it; off: nothing moves or selects by itself); and the head row IS the toolbar the owner asked for — the view switch, Live, the track-width control, and a filter by author (you / assistant / mastermind / minions) — one row, no second bar.
