## Deliverables (numbered; tick each at the end)

1. **The stage is always visible.** Every swap demo happens on a clearly bounded stage: a card with a surface (white on plain; a tint step on card) whose edges never move. The reader can point at the rectangle that will change before clicking. This is the design rule of the realm; write it in `doc/decisions.md` in one sentence: *a click changes what is inside a rectangle you could already see; the rectangle stays.*
2. **Tabs that show their panel.** The tab strip and its panel are one bounded box: the active tab connects to the panel (the panel's surface runs up under the active tab; a border or a tint frames the panel; the inactive tabs sit outside it). Do this in `paging.css` for the swap demos; if the fix belongs in `ext/tabs/tabs.css` for the whole site, write the diff as a proposal in your log and apply the local version. Prove the boundary is visible: a screenshot where a stranger can trace the panel's edge.
3. **Swap that is not tabs — four ways, same stage.** On `mechanisms/swap/`, one stage, and chips to choose the swap visual: **tabs** · **card-in** (the old card slides out, a new white card slides in — the owner's example) · **cross-fade** (the old content fades, the new appears in place, the box never changes size) · **flip** (the card turns over). Each one names what changed in a caption ("panel: Overview → Pricing"). Motion is short (≤ 250 ms) and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Then answer the owner's question on the page in two sentences: tabs are one swap visual, the mechanism is "the stage stays, the content changes", and here are three more.
4. **The mechanisms on real columns.** The hub's four miniatures stay as the ten-second explanation, but each "The real X, at full size" link lands on a page that demonstrates X with the SITE'S OWN machinery: launch = a real child column opening (routed, url changes, goes as deep as the reader clicks — three levels prepared); expand = an accordion/row that grows in place and does NOT route (say so on the page); swap = the bounded stage above, not routed; takeover = routed `full`, with the exit chip. Every page says in its first sentence what will happen when you click and whether the url changes.
5. **Tabs UX.** On the Make page's contract (read `make/` and `doc/persistence.md`; do not change the storage), add the reader-facing UX for tabs: on any made page, a "+ tab" control that adds a tab (a child page shown as a tab instead of a column), a way to rename and reorder tabs, and a chip that switches a page's children between "columns" and "tabs" presentation. Show the resulting config lines live (the code tab already does this for picks). If the persistence judge's decision lands while you work (`/imagine/paging/doc/persistence.md` will change), follow it; otherwise use the existing store contract and say so.
6. **The hub's "real X" links are right.** The owner found "The real swap, at full size" goes from `paging/` to `paging/mechanisms/swap/` — check every link on the hub lands where its sentence says, and that the landing page's first sentence matches the promise.
