# The eighth visit

I am writing down what I want **before** I ask the realm for it, so I cannot move the
goalposts afterwards.

I want **a page with a rail down the left, at reading width, in the dark, holding this note
in its box, with a whole other page running inside it** — and then I want to **throw that
page away** and find nothing left of it anywhere I can see.

Nine steps: pick it, send it, open it cold, save it, nest something in it, point it at this
file, change it from its own bar, save the nest, and unmake it.

If the last step leaves a row in the rail, a name in a parent's file, or a page that half
loads, the realm can make things it cannot take back — and every trial I run makes the next
newcomer's rail longer.
