## What the owner said the realm is for (his words)

> the idea of the paging mechanism explorer, was to be able to SEE different layouts x navigation x appearance/style x visual hierarchy in action, and maybe have a chance to configure or play with it without having to write it out as a new page. we did not really achieve that.
> utilize the whole toolbelt of navigation and ui/ux things we have (toolbars, footers, sidebars, tabs, column pages, full pages, layouts (magazines, blogs, sections, grids).. we need a library of preconfigured pages. probably with a dropdown to switch, maybe with a minimal hover-only toolbar that allows configuration. we want to be able to put any one of these page types inside any other … i'm thinking we'll want to use the ext/drawer for this.
> i want to see previews that link to demos … every single page should be accessible within a few clicks of the main paging page. I like these nav grids … multiple sections of nav grids could be useful.

## Before you design: the audit

Two critics walked the realm before you: `../paging-audit-1/task.jsonl` (the overwhelmed newcomer at 3440: what confused him, ranked, and the first screen he wished for) and `../paging-audit-1b/task.jsonl` (the systems designer: the small handful of building blocks the system actually has, what is redundant, the organizing principle). Read both logs before the first edit. Their verdicts are inputs; where you disagree, say why in `doc/decisions.md`.

**Critic A's verdict, in short:** the realm is a showroom for what happens when you click a link — the panel swaps, a column opens right, the row grows, or one page eats the screen — plus what the page looks like while it happens; he learned it by clicking, the words never said it. Five confusions, ranked: six pages are one page (Mechanisms, Styles, Sizes, Toolbars, Center, Transitions share a skeleton and differ only in which chips are lit); a control cannot be told from a word (the toolbar is bare spans; prose renders `xs`/`plain`/`full` as identical grey pills; `full`, filed under sizes, deleted the rail); one click, two columns ("The real swap" opened Mechanisms and Swap); Explorer explores nothing; the realm's own map is 2.9 screens down (the front scrolls 4776px in 1392; 54% of 3440 empty). Keep: Templates (eleven real running pages as previews — the only page understood by looking); the tile grid's behaviour (a tile replaces one column, crumbs hold, back works); Build's numbered picture-choosers with the live middle preview; Right nav (nothing moved but the middle). Also: chips persist across refresh with a "Modified" alert — decision 4 is not met today. **The first screen he wished for:** no paragraph at all; full width, four live miniatures — swap · launch · expand · takeover — each already doing its thing under one short caption; below them the Templates shapes as real pictures with one line, "pick a shape, then pick what a click does"; below that one compact grid of every page, with pictures, still above the fold; definitions, inventory, critique and the decisions one click down under Docs.

**Critic B's verdict, in short:** one rectangle and six words over it — **stage** (the box a click changes the inside of; it never moves) · **navigation** (what a click on a child does, and how children are drawn) · **content** · **room** (the width word) · **arrangement** (where the parts sit around the box) · **skin** (surface + type scale). The stage is the only block with no page, no chip and no single name (nine names in the code). Only `build/` lets a reader configure or generate; nothing crosses two blocks. Merge: navigation is written three times (keep `build/words.js`'s `NAVS`); surfaces three times; arrangement five times (`/imagine/layouts/` owns it); `build/` supersedes `make/`'s editing (`make/` = the file manager). Delete: `critique/`, `inventory/` (site surveys, not blocks), `explorer/`, `center/`, `transitions/`, the thirteen one-value directories under `styles/` `sizes/` `toolbars/` (one `route()` gives them urls), the `xs`–`xl` content axis. Missing: the two independent colour controls, a combinator crossing all six, the preset library with a dropdown, a page hosted inside a page, `ext/drawer`, the stable/dynamic label. **The principle: the realm is ONE configurable page, not a directory of pages** — six words, one stage, one control and one name each; every surviving directory is a preset that earned a url.

Build to both. The handful is critic B's six unless you can name fewer; the first screen is critic A's unless you can show something clearer.

## Deliverable 0 — the handful

The owner: *"i want the paging system to have a small handful of concrete building blocks, mechanisms, whatever... and i want to be able to see examples of them, and explore alternatives, either by generating, or configuring, or whatever."* Name at most six building blocks (the designer critic proposes them; you decide). The rebuilt realm is organized around them and nothing else: each block gets one page — what it is, SHOWN by an example; more examples as a nav grid; alternatives a reader can configure (the hover toolbar) or generate (from Make/Build's spec). Everything that is a synonym of a block merges into it; everything that is not a block and not an editor is deleted or moved under a block. The rail's first section is the handful.

## Deliverables (numbered; every one is checked against his sentences)

1. **The realm becomes an app with stable navigation.** `/imagine/paging/` opens as a `full`-width page with its own persistent LEFT RAIL (the shells realm shows the shape; do not reinvent it) and a CENTRE that swaps. The rail is the nav grids, in sections: **Library** (the preconfigured pages) · **Mechanisms** · **Templates** · **Toolbars** · **Navigation** (built by `nav-stability`; link its dir `paging/navigation/` — it may land after you; a link to a dir that exists is fine, one that does not is not, so check at the end) · **Make & Build** · **Layouts** (the sibling realm). Clicking a grid item swaps the centre; the rail never moves; the url changes to the page's url (so every page still has a url and works cold). No click ever opens two columns. At 3440 the row is used: rail + centre; no 60% dead space — measure it.
2. **The library of preconfigured pages.** A dropdown (the site's own select/menu) at the top of the centre switches among at least twelve presets, each a real, complete, themed page built from the site's toolbelt: a blog post · a magazine front · a docs page with top tabs · a dashboard with a left rail · a settings page with a right rail · a columns page · a full-screen takeover page · a section stack (sections) · a grid wall · a toolbar-top page · a toolbar-bottom page (footer) · a card-in-card nest. Each preset is a configuration (navigation × layout × surface × hierarchy) rendered live, not a screenshot; each has a plain one-line name a newcomer understands.
3. **The hover-only toolbar.** Over the stage, on hover (and on focus), a minimal toolbar appears with the few tweaks that matter: navigation type, layout number (from `/imagine/layouts/`), content surface, page background, type scale. It is invisible when you are not hovering. It replaces the floating chip rows everywhere in the realm — delete `Paging.Toolbar` as it is, or rebuild it as this. **Two independent colour controls**: the content's surface and the page/column background can each be any of the five surfaces (the owner: "card gives the content a bg, whereas the other colors change the whole column. i think we want the ability to switch either one to any color").
4. **The drawer** (`ext/drawer`) holds the rest: the full configuration as a form, the JSON, "make this a page" (hands the JSON to Make's backend — read `doc/persistence.md`; do not add a store), and **nest**: put any preset inside the current one as a child or as a referenced page, and see it.
5. **Demos do not persist.** Refresh resets. Remove the amber "modified" alert everywhere in the realm; Make and Build keep a small dot only, and a plain "Saved to disk" line. `baseline.js` shrinks to that.
6. **The swap page is tabs first.** `mechanisms/swap/` opens with a real, obvious tab strip and its panel; the alternatives (card-in, cross-fade, flip) are the next section, each introduced by doing it, not describing it. Delete "Nothing on this page navigates", "Same box", "Same width" and every caption that is a conclusion. A caption says what just changed ("Overview → Pricing").
7. **No telling before showing, anywhere in the realm.** Delete every matter-of-fact blockquote and every opening statement of the "Every page on this site is three things" kind. Where a page needs words, it is one sentence saying what to do ("Pick a preset. Hover the stage to change it.").
8. **Links land where they say.** One click = one visible change. Audit every link in the realm for the two-columns-at-once behaviour (a link to a page whose default child also opens): fix by linking to the page itself and letting nothing auto-open, or by swapping the centre. "The real swap" must land on the swap page and nothing else.
9. **Docs and the walk:** `readme.md` is the index; the hub's first screen IS the walk (rail + a preset on stage). `doc/decisions.md` records this rebuild and what it replaced, dated.
