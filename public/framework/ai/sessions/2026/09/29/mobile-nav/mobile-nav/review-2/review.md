1. [fix] `POST /ask/turn` (`Server/plugins/Ask.js:31-44`) lets anyone on the Wi-Fi start a full `claude -p` turn. The route never sets `tools`, so the turn gets every built-in tool, and `.claude/settings.json:3` is `defaultMode: "bypassPermissions"`. Any device on the LAN can therefore say "run this command" and get a shell — the same RCE that `dev/Socket/Socket.js:457` says it keeps closed. The comment at `Ask.js:26` ("no shell, no file write") is false. At minimum pass `tools: ""` plus `strict_mcp: true` (`Ask.js:150-157` explains both) and refuse a client-supplied `resume`. Better: leave the door off until the https-on-LAN proposal lands.
2. [fix] `Server/plugins/ServexProxy.js:155` forwards every method and every path under `/servex/*` to Servex, from any LAN address. That puts all of Servex's HTTP API (agents, servers, logs) on the Wi-Fi, not just the `/log/prompts` and `/api/page-ai` calls the phone needs. Allow-list those two or three paths, or guard it like `MCP.js`'s loopback check.

That is exactly the "not work and not know why" the owner named. Show the sentence as a local card with a "not sent" mark when `send()` fails, and route `servex_base()` through `servex_url()`.
