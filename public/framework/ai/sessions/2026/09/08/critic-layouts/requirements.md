## Judge — five questions, each answered with evidence, then fixed

1. **The newcomer test.** Open `/layouts/`, three layout pages (`1-flow`, `2-sidebar`, `n-wall`), two tag pages (`cards`, `2 column flex`), `/layouts/tag/` at 400 and 1920 headless (a 20-line probe in the scratchpad, `critic-layouts-*`; screenshots into this dir; Read them). Can a stranger say what each page is for in ten seconds? Is level 1 one screen — the thing shown, its parts named, the way in, room to breathe — with detail one click down? Is anything told that could be shown? Any blockquote that needs explaining? Fix the page.
2. **The words.** For every word in `layouts.json`'s `words`: is it defined in a sentence a newcomer accepts, and does it describe MANY layouts (the owner's `cards` test — a visible background with padding per column, gap usual, not required)? Are the seed words right, or did the author drift? Is the `n` branch honest? Is `4-1` the right escape hatch, and does its page say so plainly? Now the corpus: the minions invented `carousel`, `fixed sidebar`, `overlapping cards`, `right-aligned`, `scroll-pinned hero`, `no max-width`, `layout switcher`, `bot wall`, `cookie modal`, `full-bleed video`, and hit a `5-equal` footer with no id. Decide each: a layout word the encyclopedia defines (add it to `words`, with the sentence), a trait tag that needs no definition, or not a layout at all (say so in `doc/naming.md` in one line each). Never renumber; an alias for any rename, logged.
3. **The CSS answers.** On every entry, is the grid rule right, the flex rule right, and the wrap answer true (the basis threshold; grid does not wrap)? Does each show the framework's existing words first where they apply (`framework.css` utilities, `cols-row …`, the five layout words)? No new CSS class for a layout name — if one is proposed, it is a proposal in `doc/naming.md`, not shipped.
4. **The drawing.** The five wire gaps (an off-screen drawer, an arrow carousel, a footer that keeps two columns on a phone, an overlapping card strip, an infinite marquee): which are a missing spec field (add it to `Layout.js` and `doc/wire.md` only if it is one small, general field — `floor: 0` on a row may already answer the footer; check), and which are honestly outside a content-free wireframe (say so in `doc/wire.md`). Prove any `Layout.js` change bit-identical on the existing 12 wires: screenshot `/layouts/` before and after and compare.
5. **The click-through.** Every tag chip leads somewhere with content; a tag page with zero layouts AND zero sites is not listed; `/layouts/<id>/` shows the sites that use it (from `site/index.json`) and the sites that use it as a section; `/layouts/4-equal/` lands on `n-wall`. Crawl every url `/layouts/` links to at 400 and 1920: zero console errors, zero 404s, no sideways scroll.

6. **What the numbers say** (the patterns author, after counting 47 sites — `http://localhost:8123/websites/patterns/`): `1-bands` is the second most common whole-page layout (10 of 47) and the only one that survives a phone unchanged (7 of the 10 stay `1-bands`), yet `/layouts/` files `bands` as one division word among nine — should its entry and the tree say so? `n-wall` appears 12 times as a section and zero times as a whole page — its entry should say it is what you put *inside* a page. Decide both, with the numbers in the sentence, and fix the entries.
