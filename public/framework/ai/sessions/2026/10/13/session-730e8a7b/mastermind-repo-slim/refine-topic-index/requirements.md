## Deliverable

1. **One record per refined ask** in the session's own folder: `asks.jsonl` beside the session's files, appended by the refine loop (never rewritten), one line = `{at, session, prompt_ref, ask, module, status}` where `module` is the page or module the ask is about (the refiner names it from the readme index; "unknown" is allowed) and `status` starts `open`. Backfill from what the refine loop already produced for the last three days by a script with `--plan` first; say the counts.
2. **Topic indexes, computed**: `node Server/AskIndex.mjs` (`class AskIndex`) writes `<module>/doc/asks.md` for every module with asks (a short list: the ask, the session link, status), and `ai/doc/asks-by-module.md` as the overview (module, open, done); regenerated by the loop, never hand-edited. Compute, don't recall: statuses come from `asks-outcomes.md`'s judgments where they exist.
3. **Post-processing routes**: in the refine loop, after an ask is recorded: if `asks-outcomes.md` (or a landing line whose title matches) says done → status `done`, crossed off, no inbox; otherwise `ModuleInbox.add(module, ask, source)` once, and the record says `inboxed`. Tested on `ux/Dictate` with today's asks; no duplicate lines on a re-run.
4. Docs inside the module (`ext/Refine/doc/asks.md`: the record, the index, the routing, five lines each), the Refine readme's one line; card (title "Refined asks: N recorded, M crossed off, K sent to their module's inbox"); landing line checked with `tail -1`; `merge.mjs`; Servex pieces tested on a private port, the live Servex restarted by the mastermind.
