Dictation controls: five mocks

The same dictation sheet on a phone, at split size, five ways: compact, verbose, more controls, fewer controls, more space. Each one shows the same moment: you are talking, one line has been sent, and the page's feed has one event. Today's sheet has a head (AI · path, New session, More, ✕), the chat, a text box, 🎤, ⚙ and Send, and a status line. How Dictate, Refine and tasks connect.

The pick: D, fewer controls. The owner dictates on a phone while moving between pages, and this module's one promise is a mic that always shows what is happening. D makes that the biggest thing on the sheet and drops Send, because pause mode already sends each sentence. The alternative is A, compact, which keeps typing first-class in the same space. B and C add more words and switches to a sheet the owner already finds wonky; E is the best for reading back long dictation and could be D's full size. Building it is a later step.
