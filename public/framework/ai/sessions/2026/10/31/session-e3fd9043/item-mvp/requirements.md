## Earlier asks this builds on (same evening; summarized in `design/decisions-log.md` of the research repo)

- Build the Replay Lab examples as real test pages on a branch off `integration/data-shapes-2` (this branch: `claude/replay-tests`), so he can click through them.
- Simplest system that works for most cases; keep every caveat and alternative documented; no firm decision needed where it's unclear.
- A method saved as one call declares it with a silence wrapper around its body (no remembering `act`).
- Adapters that detect and upgrade old line shapes; snapshots, maybe collapsed in a build step.
