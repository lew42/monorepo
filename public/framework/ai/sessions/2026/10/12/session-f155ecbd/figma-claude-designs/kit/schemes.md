## Where each scheme is weak (from the contrast script, words only)
- **White and light gray:** strong. Accent text is weak on both, so keep small prime text on a pill. Muted text on gray sits right on the line.
- **Prime:** dark ink is fine. Muted text is below comfortable, and accent labels and bars (the `dark` mid-brown on prime) are faint: a real defect of `accent` in `Prime BG`; I did not change tokens.
- **Dark:** the ground is a mid brown, so ink is only moderate; muted and accent text are the weakest here.
- **Near-black:** the clearest dark look; a deeper ground lifts every ink.

## Quick switching
Select the page frame and change its Text mode: one click recolours every bound ground and ink. Use that for a page or template. A `Scheme` variant is only for showing two looks inside one page, and it must be edited per copy, so the mode wins.
