Sheet variants: five ways to lay out the controls

Each variant shows the same sheet at its three sizes. Pick one, or say what to take from each. proposal.md

  • A · Compact: The fewest words: a title, the page path and ✕, and one composer row. The tab row appears only at full size.
  • B · Verbose: Every control says what it does, in words, and the tabs show at split size too. It is the easiest to learn and uses the most space.
  • C · More controls: Adds a This page / Everywhere switch (which agent you are talking to), a model chip, and chips for what is selected on the page.
  • D · Fewer controls: No buttons in the head at all: you drag to resize, and ✕ appears only at full size. The mic is one big button. It is built for talking, not typing.
  • E · The live space: The sheet is the page's live feed: the chat and the page's events (a task filed, a reference, a log line) in one stream, with an All / Chat / Live switch to filter it.
