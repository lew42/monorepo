## Goal
A voice session's plugin settings (the `{Name: {enabled?, config?}}` overrides of `Recite.shared.voice_chat`, see `Interrupt`, `ThinkingCue`) are saved as lines in that session's own `session.jsonl`, `{"settings":{"<group>":{...}}}`, where `<group>` is `dictate` or `recite` (named after the classes). They replace `sessions.json` (`Servex/agents/Sessions.js`, `settings()` and `checked_plugins`, with `Servex/agents/Sessions.plugins.test.mjs`) as the home of per-session plugin settings. The session loads them by itself when it replays.
