## Scenarios (flex) — each one a numbered step list, each verified at 4 widths

1. **Split into columns**: root → right-edge click → commit → two columns. Then split the right column into two rows (bottom edge). Expect: a row of two, the second a column of two; seams visible; sizes fill.
2. **Add a third column** to the row (edge click on the middle panel's right edge). Does the new column take an equal share, or steal from one neighbour? Which one? Is that what you'd want?
3. **Resize a seam** by dragging (pointer down / move / up on the grip between column 1 and 2, 200px right). Do the sizes persist through a redraw (split again afterwards)? At 400px does the seam still reach?
4. **Set `display: flex` on a leaf**, put three swappable pieces of content in it (templates — pick three T names of different natural sizes; `random` is allowed), then toggle the flex overlay and read what it draws. Then row → column direction if the toolbar offers it. Do the children hug/fill as the words say?
5. **fill vs hug** on one axis of one column (size.js): hug a column with a short template inside — does the sibling take the rest? Then a long template — does hug grow, clip, or scroll?
6. **Nest**: a column inside a row inside a column, three deep, using only edge clicks. Then `sow` a generated seed (`space_dashboard` button) and screenshot the result at 1280 and 400: does the generated tree reflow at 400 or overflow?
7. **Drag a panel** (PanelDrag) from one column into another. Does the tree stay valid? Any duplicate or orphan?
8. **Undo/close**: close a panel; does its sibling absorb the space (`absorb()`)? Close the last child of a split — does the parent collapse back to a leaf?

For each: works / broken / unclear · what you expected vs saw · the png · the step list. Where the UI is unreachable headlessly (a hover-only control), say so and use `page.evaluate` on the Panel tree instead — and log that the UI path was the miss.
