**Laws first** (CLAUDE.md): less is more — ASAP; clarity is the one exception; prioritize. **Length budget:** the deliverable is ONE page — `page.js` in this task dir — showing six before/after screenshot pairs and the exact patch; ≤ 8 log lines. **No edits outside this task dir and the scratchpad.** Never drive the owner's live browser tabs. `ext/Panel/**` is another session's.

## The proposal you are previewing

Read [`../page-layout-audit/proposal.md`](../page-layout-audit/proposal.md) §5 and [`../page-layout-audit/inventory.md`](../page-layout-audit/inventory.md) §2–3 first. In one sentence: `Page.css`'s grid moves from bare `.page {` to `.page.standard {` (5 rules: `Page.css:78`, `:99-101`, `:279`); `.page.standard` stops declaring `--measure: 40em` and reads `var(--measure, 40em)`; every `--measure: none` (`ext/tabs/tabs.css:68`, `ext/demo/app.css:58`, `ext/DesignTool/DesignTool.css:77`, `styles/layouts/layouts.css:19`, `ext/Doc/Doc.css:14`) becomes `100%`; `.page.full` (`:240`) and `.page.solo` (`:112`) are deleted; seven call sites gain the word `standard` (`ext/Doc/Doc.js` ×3 `c("page doc-page")` → `c("page standard doc-page")`, four `classes: "dt-page"` → `"standard dt-page"`).
