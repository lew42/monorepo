## Find the cause before the fix — these are the suspects; measure each

1. **Many roots, one rail.** `selection()` and `land()` are per ROOT. `land()` clears `.focus` only inside the target's `.panel-workspace`; a panel focused in ANOTHER root (the demo's other pane, an inline demo on the Panel page, a second document) keeps `root.focus` AND its ring while the rail moves on. Escape then drops whichever roots hear it. The invariant has to be page-wide: **one selection across every root on the page**. The cleanest shape is that every root listens to `panel-focus` and lets go (delete `root.focus`, clear its own rings, `root.emit("focus", null)` — without re-announcing) when the detail's `root()` is not itself. No registry.
2. **Stale `$root`.** `drop()` clears rings inside `$root.el`, and `workspace.js`'s `draw()` rebuilds the DOM from the tree on add/remove — if the `$root` a `selection()` closed over is no longer the live box, the ring in the live box survives the drop. `listen()` unbinds on `!isConnected` — so in that case Escape silently does nothing for that root. Check whether `mount()`/`draw()` rewire `selection()` for the new DOM, and make the ring-clearing find the LIVE views (`views.get(item)` — paint.js — is the door properties.js uses; or query from the document rather than a captured box).
3. **Many views of one root.** `view()` paints `.focus` at construction when `item.id === root.focus`; `land()` adds it to ONE view. The playground shows one of seven boxes at a time — the ring must be on the panel in whichever box is visible, so either every view of the focused panel wears it (then "exactly one ring" means one per view of that panel — state this in the doc) or the visible one does. Measure what happens on a viewport-mode switch.
4. **Inspector panels** (`template: properties`, `focus: true`) draw the rail INSIDE a panel and never take focus — a click on one must not change the selection or the ring; a second rail must not fight the docked one. Check `inspects()` is honoured on every path (text.js's click, the groups drill).
5. **"Clicking off."** Today a click never deselects (Escape and `panel-unfocus` do). The owner clicked "off" expecting the ring to go. Decide the smallest rule that is still safe — a click on a surface that is neither a panel nor a control (not the rail/drawer, not a bar, not a top-layer popover, not the DevBar) drops the selection — or decide against and say why in `doc/focus.md`; either way, the ring must NEVER outlive the rail's "nothing selected".

## Build

- **The invariant, enforced in one place** (`focus.js`): one selected panel per page; `land()`/`drop()` clear rings page-wide through the live views; roots release on another root's `panel-focus`. Delete guards that the invariant makes unnecessary.
- **Hover = "this is what a click selects."** A hovered panel shows a clear ring — distinct from the focus ring (the owner: "each panel's border should change a bit"; strong, not subtle — e.g. a 2px ring in a lighter prim or `--line` darkened, inset like the focus ring so layout never moves) — on exactly the panel `drill()` would land on: inside an unopened group the GROUP lights, inside the open group its child, the selected panel itself shows no extra hover. CSS cannot express the drill (it depends on `root.focus`), so it is a JS class (`panel-hover`) set on `mouseover` from the same `drill()` the click uses — one function, two readers — in a NEW `focus.css` loaded by focus.js (`View.stylesheet(import.meta, "focus.css")`; `@layer theme`; prefix `panel-`; register the class in `styles/css-scopes.txt` if that file lists Panel's). Leave panel.css's `.focus::after` rule where it is (flow-sizing's file) — note in your log that the ring rule belongs in focus.css next and should move when that task has landed.
- **The `+` on hover only** (`repeat.css`): `.panel-repeat-add` is a real flow sibling — keep its slot, hide it (`opacity`/`visibility`) until its panel is the hovered target under the same group gate; no layout shift on hover (measure the grid's rects before/after).
