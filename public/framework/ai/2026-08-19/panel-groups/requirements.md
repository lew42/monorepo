## The shape (the accepted strategy says "never a fourth Panel subclass" — a group is a RULE on a split, not a class)

- **Every split can be a group; a `group` word (on | off) on a split says whether it is one.** Default: the root's direct children in `mode: document` (the sections) are groups; everything else is off (today's behaviour — innermost wins). One `WORDS` row in `glyphs.js` (`modes: split-only` — check how `live_words` gates; if WORDS cannot express "splits only", one line in `properties.js` beside the `mode` root row — say which).
- **Selection drills down.** Today `workspace.js:147` focuses the panel whose bar/body you clicked (innermost). New rule in `focus.js` (`focus(item, $panel)` is the one writer): the click resolves to the **outermost unselected group** on the path from the clicked panel up to the root — if no group on the path is selected, select the outermost group; if the clicked panel is inside the currently focused group, go one level deeper (the next group, or the leaf); a click on the focused panel itself keeps it. Escape goes one level OUT (if `focus.js` already has Escape = unfocus, make it step out first, unfocus at the top).
- **Hover wakes only inside a selected group.** The `innermost wins` CSS idiom (`toolbar.css:46-56`, `split.css:24`, `display.css:50-51`, tools) gates the bar, the edges and the overlay on `:hover:not(:has(.panel:hover))`. Add one guard: inside a `.panel-group` that is not `.focus` and has no `.focus` descendant, children get no hover affordances — their `.panel-edge`, `.panel-bar`, `.panel-display` stay inert (`pointer-events: none` / `opacity: 0`), and the GROUP's own bar/edges show instead. One CSS block in `panel.css` (or a new `group.css` if it is more than ~15 lines — prefer panel.css), `panel-group` class written by `size.js`'s `sizing()` (it is the one writer of a panel's own classes) or `paint.js` — say which, one writer.
- **Drag a whole section.** `PanelDrag.js` — verify a group (a split) drags as one, with its children, by its own bar handle; if today only leaves drag, make the group's bar the handle (`PanelDrag` extends `Sortable`; the handle selector is where). Measure: after the drag, `root.toJSON()` has the same panel count and the moved subtree intact.
