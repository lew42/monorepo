Last in the whole rail, and the most complex demo in the module: the `acme` tree
is both — a landing home of five bands, plus a three-section, up-to-three-levels
deep documentation area (`Docs → Install/Pages/Deploy`, `Pricing`, `About`) — all
one tree, all the techniques from the thirteen demos before it composed at once.

## Why it comes last

Every idea it uses was taught in isolation earlier in the rail: bands
(`landing`), a section whose content *is* its children (`children/`, `handbook`),
crumbs from depth (`add`, `deep`). Nothing here is new; the point of ending on it
is proving the pieces actually compose into something that reads as one real
site, not a pile of independent tricks.
