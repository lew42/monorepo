# OpenRouter Pricing

## Pricing Structure
OpenRouter pricing has three broad paths: Free, Pay-as-you-go, and Enterprise. You pay only for what you use with access to 500+ AI models.

## Token-Based Pricing
You pay each model's token rate, passed through at the provider's published price with no markup. OpenRouter adds a platform fee when you load credits. Each model has separate input (prompt) and output (completion) token prices.

## Platform Fee
The published fee schedule sets that fee at **5.5%**, with a **$0.80 minimum** per purchase and **5% for crypto**.

## Free Models
OpenRouter offers dozens of free models with zero per-token cost. Free models have rate limits (20 req/min, 200 req/day) but require no credits to use.

## No Minimums or Monthly Fees
There are no monthly fees or minimum commitments.

## BYOK Option
Bring your own provider key, and the fee falls to 5% of the normal cost after the first million requests a month.

## Enterprise Tier
Enterprise tier is custom-priced and adds:
- SSO/SAML
- Contractual SLAs
- Priority support
- Dedicated support channels

## Model Pricing
OpenRouter offers 1292 models with published API pricing. Input prices range from $0.017 to $150.00 per million tokens, with the cheapest being Granite 4.0 Micro at $0.017 per million input tokens.
