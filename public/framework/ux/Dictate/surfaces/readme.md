# Surfaces

The one chat, [`ux/Dictate/chat.js`](/framework/ux/Dictate/), is live at the top. Below it are the five places the site mounts it, each shown with a screenshot and the one call it makes. Every place shares one global voice session, so a conversation started in one shows up in all of them (CLAUDE.md law 6, "one of everything").

## Use

Type or dictate into the box at the top, then open the ✦ (phone) or the ☰ (desktop): the same conversation is there.

## Watch out

- Each call shown is the short form. The full call, with its placeholder, is in the linked file.
- The screenshots are from 2026-09-30 (one-dictation, merge 2). Take new ones when a surface changes.
