The owner approved archiving the test and lab masters ("archive, sure"). Archive means **move, never delete**: nothing was removed, every instance stays linked, and every step is undone by moving the node back, unhiding it or renaming it. This clears blockers 4 and 8 of the [publish audit](./research/publish-audit.md). File: [Lew42 v2](https://www.figma.com/design/ADpu9u2V0BlEZfR3rrMfER/Lew42-v2), `ADpu9u2V0BlEZfR3rrMfER`. 1600 LG was not touched (another agent was editing it).
