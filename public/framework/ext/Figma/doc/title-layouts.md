# Title layouts: where the words go (owner, 2026-10-12 night)

A title card (Netflix / Prime Video key art, a video title slide, a T-shirt) is one of the most basic layout questions: given 2, 3, 4 or 5 words, where does each word go, how big is it, and how do the lines lock together?

## The owner's observations
- Small "filler" words (the, of, a, and, in) can be **smaller** and placed **strategically** (tucked between, beside or above the big words), so the main words carry the weight: "The Lord of the Rings" reads as LORD · RINGS with "the … of the" as small connectors.
- **Two lines lock together**: in auto layout, the top line aligns to its bottom and the bottom line to its top, so the pair meets in the middle with a tight, deliberate gap.
- Alignment is a choice per layout: centred both ways, left or right, top or bottom, or a mix.
- The goal is a set of robust, reusable **text layout schematics** (a layout works for many titles), for mobile (Shorts, 9:16) and video presentation cards (16:9).
- "I won't know how I'd judge it until I see it": show many variations side by side.
