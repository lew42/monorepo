Why every threshold in `rules.js`/`polish.js` is a dimensionless ratio rather
than a pixel count, and the walkthrough of the six that matter most — the
frame gap chief among them, "the measurement the tool exists for."

## It's the conceptual companion to `knowledge/thresholds.md`

This file explains *why* a ratio (the general principle, "find what the
measurement should be relative to, and divide"); `thresholds.md` is the actual
table of every number and its calibration source. A reader wanting the reason
starts here; a reader wanting a specific cutoff goes there.
