DESIGN INTENT — deliberate choices on this site. None of these is a defect. Report one
only if it is applied *wrongly* here, never merely because it is present.

- The reading column is capped at **40em** (`--measure`) so prose stays ~80 characters.
  Space to its right is the grid's `wide` track left empty on purpose. Widening the
  measure is never the fix; a second region is. Do not report "dead space on the right".
- Muted text (`--subtle`) is a deliberate tier and already clears 4.5:1. Do not report
  grey secondary text as low contrast unless it is genuinely illegible in the image.
- `.wall` sets `align-items: start`: cards in a row are as tall as their content, and a
  ragged bottom edge is intended. A part-filled last row is normal.
- The **left app sidebar is chrome**, present on every page — not the subject. Say nothing
  about its links, gear, or circle unless the region IS the sidebar.
- A short label above blank space is usually a **caption under its own thumbnail**, and
  thumbnails are opt-in — a preview that is absent was never requested, not a failed render.
