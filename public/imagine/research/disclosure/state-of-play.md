# The State of Play, 2026

**The latest official numbers.** AARO's [FY2025 Consolidated Annual Report](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/FY25%20UAP%20Annual%20Report/AARO_FY2025_Consolidated_Annual_Report_on_UAP.pdf), released [~Jul 20 2026](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/aaro-releases-fy2025-uap-report-months-after-statutory-deadline-citing-319-new-cases-and-expanded-satellite-analysis/) and months past its statutory deadline, covers June 2024–May 2025: 319 new reports (274 air-domain, 44 space-domain, 1 maritime-domain). AARO resolved 114 of them — to balloons, birds, satellites, aircraft, drones, a rocket launch, and one manned jet pack. Zero were attributed to non-human origin. *(established)*

**The unresolved backlog.** Roughly 40 percent of AARO's overall case backlog remains formally unresolved for lack of data. Advocates read that persistence itself as the finding; AARO frames it as a sensor-and-process gap, the same limitation the 2021 ODNI report named five years earlier (see [The 2021 ODNI Assessment](./odni-2021.md)). *(contested)*

**The one open case worth watching.** The FY2025 report's single maritime-domain incident: Navy assets off Virginia's coast [reported roughly 100 airborne UAP plus two likely uncrewed surface systems](https://defensescoop.com/2026/07/21/pentagon-investigating-ufo-uap-event-near-virginia-coast/). AARO confirms it is actively investigating with the reporting unit; there is no conclusion as of the report date — the most concrete open thread in the entire 2026 record. *(contested)*

**Institutional response.** AARO [stood up an outside science advisory council in 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/06/17/new-science-advisory-council-forms-to-help-us-government-resolve-the-uap-mystery/), directly echoing NASA's 2023 recommendation to bring independent scientists into case review — a sign the "more rigor, not more secrecy" camp inside government is still gaining ground. *(established)*

**Two narratives, same data.** Advocacy framing (Grusch, *Age of Disclosure*, some members of Congress): decades of institutional secrecy are finally cracking, and named officials are confirming it. Official framing (AARO, Kirkpatrick, NASA): the resolution rate keeps climbing (114/319 this cycle, similar ratios before), every tested physical claim has come back mundane, and the unresolved remainder tracks sensor and reporting gaps rather than a hidden phenomenon. Both narratives point at the same 40-percent backlog and read it oppositely. *(contested — this is the actual fault line of the whole arc, not any single incident)*

**What would settle the entire arc.** Either the open Virginia maritime case resolving to a named mundane cause — as 114 of 319 did this cycle, and 118 of 757 did the cycle before — or AARO/the intelligence community producing one piece of physical, independently testable evidence. Nothing else has moved the trajectory since the 2017 NYT story.
