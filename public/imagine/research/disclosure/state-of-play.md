# The State of Play, 2026

**The latest official numbers.** AARO's [FY2025 Consolidated Annual Report](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/FY25%20UAP%20Annual%20Report/AARO_FY2025_Consolidated_Annual_Report_on_UAP.pdf), released [~Jul 20 2026](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/aaro-releases-fy2025-uap-report-months-after-statutory-deadline-citing-319-new-cases-and-expanded-satellite-analysis/) and months past its statutory deadline, covers June 2024–May 2025: 319 new reports (274 air-domain, 44 space-domain, 1 maritime-domain). AARO resolved 114 of them — to balloons, birds, satellites, aircraft, drones, a rocket launch, and one manned jet pack. Zero were attributed to non-human origin. *(established)*

**The unresolved backlog.** Roughly 40 percent of AARO's overall case backlog remains formally unresolved for lack of data. Advocates read that persistence itself as the finding; AARO frames it as a sensor-and-process gap, the same limitation the 2021 ODNI report named five years earlier (see [The 2021 ODNI Assessment](./odni-2021.md)). *(contested)*

**The one open case worth watching.** The FY2025 report's single maritime-domain incident: Navy assets off Virginia's coast [reported roughly 100 airborne UAP plus two likely uncrewed surface systems](https://defensescoop.com/2026/07/21/pentagon-investigating-ufo-uap-event-near-virginia-coast/). The public record is one paragraph — no incident date, no named ship or unit, no sensor type. The leading mundane candidate: the Navy's own Global Autonomous Reconnaissance Craft, uncrewed boats run in NATO's Dynamic Messenger and UNITAS drills along the East Coast that same season, against a Norfolk-area baseline of up to 606 peak commercial drone flights — the same kind of dense, ordinary air-and-sea traffic a 2019 California swarm partly resolved to (a Hong Kong-flagged commercial vessel). Sen. Kaine read it as a detection-capability gap; Sen. Gillibrand called the report itself a crucial step forward. Neither settles the case. *(contested)*

**Institutional response — the science council, twice over.** AARO [stood up an outside science advisory council in 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/06/17/new-science-advisory-council-forms-to-help-us-government-resolve-the-uap-mystery/), echoing NASA's 2023 recommendation. Then, separately, the White House, AARO, ODNI and the FBI jointly formed a *UAP Science Advisory Council* and asked Harvard's Avi Loeb — the Galileo Project's public face — to chair it (see [The Galileo Project](./galileo-project/)). Academic and government tracks that ran independently through round 1 have converged. *(established)*

**The bigger institutional shift: PURSUE.** In Feb 2026 Trump ordered federal agencies to declassify UAP and extraterrestrial-life files; the resulting PURSUE Task Force has since released five tranches at war.gov/UFO to over a billion visits — the fastest-moving disclosure channel in the whole arc, moving on executive order rather than the NDAA fight. The people who lobbied for this for years are also the ones saying the files, once delivered, still are not enough to work with. Full thread: [PURSUE: the 2026 Disclosure Order](./pursue-disclosure/). *(established)*

**Two narratives, same data.** Advocacy framing (Grusch, *Age of Disclosure*, some members of Congress): decades of institutional secrecy are finally cracking, and named officials are confirming it. Official framing (AARO, Kirkpatrick, NASA): the resolution rate keeps climbing (114/319 this cycle, similar ratios before), every tested physical claim has come back mundane, and the unresolved remainder tracks sensor and reporting gaps rather than a hidden phenomenon. Both narratives point at the same 40-percent backlog and read it oppositely. *(contested — this is the actual fault line of the whole arc, not any single incident)*

**What would settle the entire arc.** Either the open Virginia maritime case resolving to a named mundane cause — as 114 of 319 did this cycle, and 118 of 757 did the cycle before, at confidence levels that vary case by case (see [Two Resolved Cases, Read Side by Side](./resolved-cases/)) — or AARO/the intelligence community producing one piece of physical, independently testable evidence. PURSUE has changed *how fast* files move; nothing has yet moved that trajectory since the 2017 NYT story.
