# The AATIP Leak and the Navy Videos

**The story.** On Dec 16 2017 the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/12/16/us/politics/pentagon-program-ufo-harry-reid.html) revealed the Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP) — a $22M Pentagon effort [requested by Sen. Harry Reid in 2007](https://fas.org/publication/aatip-list/), run partly out of Robert Bigelow's Las Vegas facility. Luis Elizondo, who led it, resigned in protest of excess secrecy and brought reporter Leslie Kean the story. This is the moment UAP left tabloid territory for mainstream journalism and Congress. *(established)*

**The videos.** Three infrared clips leaked via Tom DeLonge's To The Stars Academy years earlier — FLIR1 (~2004-2015 vintage), GIMBAL and GOFAST (both 2015) — were officially [declassified and confirmed authentic by the Pentagon in April 2020](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/pentagon-declassifies-3-ufo-videos-after-blink-182s-tom-delonge-leaked-them/). Confirming a video is real is not the same as confirming what it shows. *(established)*

**The event behind FLIR1: Nimitz, 2004.** ~100 miles off San Diego, radar operator [Kevin Day tracked objects](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony) descending 80,000ft in under a second. F/A-18 pilots [David Fravor and Alex Dietrich intercepted](https://www.history.com/articles/uss-nimitz-2004-tic-tac-ufo-encounter) a 40ft wingless white "Tic Tac" that seemed to mirror their movements, then reappeared at a rendezvous point 60 miles away minutes later. *(contested — the radar and visual accounts are not independently corroborated by public raw data)*

**The mundane accounting.** Skeptic [Mick West's video-by-video reconstruction](https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-skeptics-guide-to-the-pentagons-ufo-videos/), also laid out at [UAPedia](https://www.uapedia.ai/wiki/flir-gimbal-gofast-an-explainer/): FLIR1 is a distant plane whose apparent motion is camera pan; GIMBAL is glare from two jet engines, its "rotation" a diffraction-spike artifact crossed by parallax as the tracking pod rolls; GOFAST is a slow balloon whose ground-relative parallax creates an illusion of speed. All three explained without invoking novel physics or sensor error. *(contested)*

**The sober case something persists.** The pilots reject the reconstruction on its own terms: no wings, no exhaust plume, no visible propulsion, and — per Fravor and Underwood — kinematics (instant reappearance at a predetermined coordinate) that outrun a balloon or a jet under any known model. That is a first-hand, on-the-record account from trained observers, not an anonymous tip. *(contested)*

**What would settle it.** Raw, unprocessed multi-sensor data for the same event — calibrated radar range/velocity gates alongside the EO/IR feed, independently reprocessed by an uninvolved third party — rather than a single degraded infrared clip. Neither side has ever published that for FLIR1, GIMBAL, or GOFAST.
