# PURSUE: the 2026 Disclosure Order

Round 1 closed before this thread existed. In Feb 2026 the executive branch, not Congress, became the fastest-moving disclosure channel — worth its own chapter.

**The trigger.** On a podcast with Brian Tyler Cohen, Barack Obama said aliens are real, I have not seen them; after backlash he [clarified](https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/16/politics/obama-clarifies-alien-comments-scli-intl) that he saw no evidence during his presidency of extraterrestrial contact. Three days later, [Trump directed](https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/19/politics/aliens-ufos-trump) the Secretary of War and other agencies to begin identifying and releasing government files on UAP, UFOs, and extraterrestrial life. *(established)*

**The machine.** The Department of War stood up the PURSUE (Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters) [Task Force](https://defensescoop.com/2026/05/14/uap-trump-first-pursue-ufo-file-drop/) on May 1 2026, publishing everything at war.gov/UFO — no clearance required. Five tranches shipped roughly monthly through the summer; the portal passed one billion visits. Later tranches carried markedly less material than the first, read by some as the least-sensitive content being released first. On Jul 31 2026 [ODNI issued guidance](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/08/odni-issues-preliminary-guidance-on-disclosure-of-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena) freeing IC employees from old non-disclosure agreements — but only to report to a designated PURSUE representative, not to the public or Congress outside existing channels. *(established)*

**The reaction, from people who wanted this.** [Christopher Mellon](https://defensescoop.com/2026/05/14/uap-trump-first-pursue-ufo-file-drop/): data alone is not disclosure, releasing raw files without context may confuse more than clarify. Rear Adm. Tim Gallaudet: without metadata it is impossible to conclude any object was truly anomalous. Alejandro Rojas of Enigma Labs: case summaries without coordinates, sensor parameters, or altitude data make independent analysis very difficult. The people asking for this for years are the same people saying the files, once delivered, are not enough to work with. *(contested)*

**One thread it touched: Lake Huron.** An octagonal object shot down over Lake Huron on Feb 12 2023 was never recovered at the time. [Declassified 2026 footage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Lake_Huron_high-altitude_object) plus an earlier document trail point to a weather- or hobbyist pico-balloon, with a Canadian defence report flagging a US National Weather Service radar station as the likely launch site — a probable answer, arrived at only because PURSUE forced the file open, not a closed one. *(established)*

**What would settle whether PURSUE is disclosure or a data dump.** The same events, re-released with sensor metadata, coordinates, and multi-source correlation attached — not degraded video and redacted PDFs alone. Whether the task force ever does that, or keeps shipping progressively thinner tranches, is the actual test, and it has not resolved either way as of this writing.
