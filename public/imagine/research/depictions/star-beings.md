# Hopi & Anasazi star-beings — Sego Canyon and the Blue Star Kachina

**The artifact.** [Sego Canyon](https://www.ancient-origins.net/ancient-places-americas/haunting-rock-art-sego-canyon-extra-terrestrials-or-spiritual-visions-001584) (Utah) layers three rock-art traditions on one sandstone wall: Archaic-period (8000–500 BCE) Barrier Canyon Style figures — limbless, hollow-eyed anthropomorphs up to nine feet tall — plus later Fremont (600–1250 CE) and historic Ute imagery. Separately, Hopi prophecy describes a "Blue Star Kachina" (Saquasohuh), a being whose appearance in the sky is said to herald the Fifth World. *(established)*

**What the culture's own context says.** Archaeologists read the armless torsos, vacant "bug" eyes, and vertical body markings as [entoptic/shamanic imagery](https://www.greatamericanhikes.com/post/the-haunting-rock-art-of-sego-canyon) tied to ritual and hallucinogen use — a Barrier Canyon style attested across the Colorado Plateau well before Fremont or Hopi culture existed. Kachinas (katsinas) are, in Hopi religion, real spirit-intermediaries embodied in dance and ceremony, not a claim about literal sky visitors. *(established)*

**The alien interpretation.** History Channel's *Ancient Aliens* (Giorgio Tsoukalos) frames Sego Canyon's ghostly figures as a record of extraterrestrial contact. A parallel modern claim reads the [Blue Star Kachina prophecy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Star_Kachina) as forecasting a real celestial or alien event. *(fringe)*

**What a fair reader keeps.** The prophecy itself is the genuinely odd object here — just not for the reason claimed. Researcher Jason Colavito traced the popular Blue Star Kachina prophecy text and found no version predating the late 20th century: it looks like a modern text retrofitted onto real Hopi katsina tradition and then cited as if it were ancient corroboration. *(contested)*

**What would settle it.** For Sego Canyon, style-dating already places Barrier Canyon imagery millennia before Hopi culture existed — close to dispositive on its own. For the Blue Star Kachina, publishing the earliest documented source of the prophecy text (versus its claimed oral-tradition origin) would settle whether it predates 20th-century New Age literature. **Round 2 — not a one-off.** The Blue Star Kachina's recency signature — a claim of deep antiquity with a documented first appearance clustered in the 20th century — turns out to be a recognizable move, not a fluke: the Dogon "Sirius mystery," the Emerald Tablets of Thoth, and the Kolbrin Bible all show the same pattern.
